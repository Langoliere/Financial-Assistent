# Safety rules

- This is a forecasting service, not a trading bot.
- Do not connect to brokers.
- Do not place orders.
- Do not provide investment advice.
- Do not implement automatic trading.
- Do not deploy to cloud services.
- Do not run git push.
- Do not run git commit unless explicitly requested.
- Do not delete files unless explicitly requested.
- Do not download data from the internet.
- Do not retrain models automatically.
- Do not store secrets in source code.
